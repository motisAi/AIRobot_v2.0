Not fitted: SIM7600X 4G modem, IMU, wheel encoders, wheels, lidar, ultrasonic, INMP441 I²S mics (planned as the durable replacement for USB audio).

Notes / cautions

  • Servo power must come from the dedicated 5–6 V supply, never the Pi's 5V rail.
  • I2C on the ESP32-S3 is GPIO 8 (SDA) / GPIO 9 (SCL) — the classic-ESP32 GPIO22 pin does not exist on the S3.
  • Two mics, two roles, two devices. Wake and command must never resolve to the same ALSA device (single-mic handoff failed — bug_055). Devices are resolved by stable identity (by-id / card id), never by index.
  • Tablet vendor RAM spec ("10/15 GB") is virtual — treat as 4 GB real; keep the face app lightweight (2D Canvas, vanilla JS).
