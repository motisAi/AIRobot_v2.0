Hardware — Wiring & Connection Map (canonical)

The single source of truth for every physical connection on Stella: ports, pins, buses, power and the config keys that depend on them. Update it whenever something is plugged in, moved or rewired. Other docs (bom.md, ../schematics/system-overview.md, ../firmware/hand-esp32.md, the briefs) describe parts and data-flow; wiring lives here.

Last verified: camera, mics, fan, eyes pinout, network on 2026-10-27; hand on 2026-08-15. Items marked ⚠ verify were taken from datasheets/overlays, not measured on the robot.

4. Robotic hand — ESP32-S3 + PCA9685 (unchanged)

Pi 5 ──USB (CH343)──> ESP32-S3-WROOM-1 ──I2C 0x40──> PCA9685 ──PWM ~50 Hz──> 5× finger servos
                                                        ▲ V+ from a SEPARATE 5–6 V supply, common GND
Link Detail
Pi → ESP32-S3 USB serial /dev/ttyACM0, 115200, newline ASCII (firmware/hand/hand.ino, driver parts_used/esp32_hand.py)
ESP32-S3 → PCA9685 I²C addr 0x40, SDA = GPIO 8, SCL = GPIO 9 (on the S3)
PCA9685 power logic VCC from ESP32 3V3; servo V+ from an external 5–6 V supply, never the Pi; common ground
Channels 0 pinky · 1 ring · 2 middle · 3 index · 4 thumb (open {1500,1500,1500,1500,2000} · closed {2600,2600,2600,2600,500} µs)
Boot homes to a closed fist (~2.5 s). Stop airobot before reflashing (it holds the port)

5. Hand + Eyes — ONE ESP32-S3 (corrected 2026-10-31)

The hand and the eyes are the same ESP32-S3 (not a separate C3 — that was wrong). One board drives the PCA9685 (hand) over I²C and the two GC9D01 eye LCDs over SPI.

Pi 5 ──USB 115200──> ESP32-S3 ──I2C  (GPIO8 SDA / GPIO9 SCL)──> PCA9685 @0x40 ──> 5 hand servos
                              └──SPI (DIN4/CLK7/DC10)──────────> 2× GC9D01 160×160 eyes

The S3 DevKit has two type-C ports: a native-USB one (usb-Espressif_*) and a CH343 UART one (usb-1a86_*, GPIO43/44). We drive everything over one of them; the firmware's CDC_ON_BOOT picks which (0 = CH343/UART0, 1 = native-USB CDC). Current build: CH343 (the hand flashes and runs reliably there). MAC 30:ED:A0:BD:2E:64.

Function Module pin ESP32-S3 GPIO Note
Hand I²C SDA GPIO8 PCA9685 @0x40 (scan also shows 0x70 all-call)
Hand I²C SCL GPIO9
Eyes SPI DIN/SDA GPIO4 TFT_MOSI 4 (shared)
Eyes SPI CLK/SCL GPIO7 TFT_SCLK 7 (shared)
Eyes DC GPIO10 TFT_DC 10 — off 8/9 so the PCA keeps them
Eyes RST1 (left) GPIO11 TFT_RST 11 — NOT GPIO0 (S3 boot-strap → boot loop)
Eyes RST2 (right) GPIO5 TFT2_RST 5
Eyes CS1 (left) GPIO2 sketch toggles CS (TFT_CS -1)
Eyes CS2 (right) GPIO6
Eyes BL1 (left) GPIO1 HIGH = on
Eyes BL2 (right) GPIO3 HIGH = on
Power VCC 3V3 module is 3.3V/5V; logic 3.3V. Use a GOOD 3V3 pin — a dead one read 2.6 V and nothing lit
Power GND GND shared

Pin trade-off: eyes DC/RST1 sit on GPIO10/11 so the PCA9685 keeps GPIO8/9. The other option is Waveshare's reference DC=9/RST1=8 with the PCA I²C moved to GPIO12/13 (ESP32 I²C remaps to any free pin). Pick one; don't let the eyes and the PCA share 8/9.

Firmware: firmware/stella_s3/stella_s3.ino = combined hand + eyes, one serial line, routed by first char (lowercase words → hand; G/B/C/E/P → eyes). Hand protocol: fist|open|hello|middle| point|peace|thumbs|count N|set 11111|f/m/s <ch> …|speed|off|pos. Eyes: G x y·B·C·E n·P n. Vendored display driver at firmware/libraries/TFT_eSPI. Build/flash recipe: firmware/README.md.

⚠ Must build with arduino-esp32 2.0.x (PlatformIO platform = espressif32@6.5.0, or Arduino IDE ESP32 core 2.0.x). On arduino-esp32 3.x the vendored TFT_eSPI 2.5.0 crashes in tft.init() (begin_tft_write, StoreProhibited) and boot-loops — which looked just like "dim blue, no image." Flash from a PC with esptool 5.x (4.5.1 fails the baud change over the S3 USB-serial/JTAG).

⚠ Status 2026-10-31: hand WORKS (PCA on 8/9, homes to fist). Eyes not yet confirmed lit — the boot-loop crash above masked everything; retest after the arduino-2.x rebuild (the panels/wiring may be fine). The user also planned to swap this S3 in case it is damaged (it has a dead 3V3 pin + prior short).

Pi side: modules/vision/eye_tracker.py (EyeController), hand parts_used/esp32_hand.py; one shared serial link when both are enabled (config hardware.eyes_*, hand.serial_port).

Power: ~100–200 mA from the Pi's USB is fine for the eyes alone.
